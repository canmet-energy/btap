## D-105 — Embedded ground-floor systems require full-area insulation

**Decided:** A floor-on-ground with modeled evidence of embedded heating
ducts, cables, or heating/cooling pipes is assigned the Table 3.2.3.1
transmittance over its full area in every heating-degree-day category.
Sentence 3.2.3.3.(4), not the ordinary-floor option in Sentence (3), controls.
This is a ruled runtime choice, not an AHJ referral.

**Applicability:** the public prescriptive applier and the performance-path
reference builder use the same inventory. A ground floor is detected when it
has a `ConstructionWithInternalSource`. For the three supported
low-temperature radiant zone-object classes, OpenStudio derives `surfaces()`
from that construction; the view associates the equipment with the same
construction evidence and is not an independent proof. The performance path
takes the inventory from the proposed model before reference-HVAC replacement
and carries surface names to the clone. OpenStudio changes object handles when
cloning, preserves names, and enforces case-insensitive name uniqueness. A
floor-radiant object assigned to a zone but resolving no served surface makes
the zone's ground-floor applicability unresolved unless the construction
evidence already identifies one. The tool then warns, leaves the floor as
modeled, and does not guess that Sentence (3)'s perimeter strip applies.

For a detected floor, the direct prescriptive transform preserves the
internal-source construction and its source location while changing its
insulation layer. Internal-source walls and roofs are also valid layered
assemblies for Table 3.2.2.2 retargeting and the reference lightweight
rebuild, but they do not classify a ground floor: only ground floors enter
this inventory. Where a Kiva foundation is present, Sentence (5)'s required
depth is measured from the foundation-wall top to the floor bottom. The tool
adds the maximum adjoining Foundation-wall height to the final floor
construction thickness, and compares an existing exterior vertical-insulation
exception against that same depth. Otherwise the full-area insulation is
continued vertically to that level. A missing foundation or unresolvable
floor or wall depth produces a surface-specific warning rather than a false
claim that the continuation was modeled.

**Who/when:** Sol, 2026-10-16.

**Correction (Sol, 2026-10-17):** Fable's implementation review established
that OpenStudio's radiant `surfaces()` result is derived from the
internal-source construction rather than corroborating it independently, and
that Kiva vertical depth is measured from the wall top rather than the slab
top. The evidence description and Sentence (5) implementation were corrected
accordingly; the Sentence (4) full-area ruling is unchanged.

**Why:** Sentence (3) expressly applies to floors with **no** embedded system
and permits the table value over a 1.2 m perimeter strip or the full area,
according to Table 3.2.3.1. Sentence (4) is the converse case and requires
both the table transmittance and insulation under the full area. Deciding
extent from HDD alone therefore silently misclassified embedded floors in
zones 4 through 7B; zone 8 happened to reach the same extent for a different
reason. Absence of a resolvable surface relationship is not evidence that an
indicated radiant system is absent.

**Legacy finding:** the pinned `openstudio-standards` revision
`f01da13a6b89e45761d1ede481fedb1c1aeb6ea0` provides useful mechanics in
`Prototype.hvac_systems.rb`: it assigns each radiant object to exact surfaces
and uses `ConstructionWithInternalSource` for embedded floors. Its
ASHRAE-climate R-15-to-R-25 insulation policy is not the NECB table rule, and
its NECB envelope path independently makes the ordinary floor decision from
HDD alone. The surface-association mechanism was consulted; neither legacy
policy was adopted.

**Evidence:** `test_embedded_ground_floor_is_full_area_in_both_editions`
constructs a real SDK electric radiant floor, pins the full-area target,
retained source construction, Sentence (5) continuation, and D-105 audit in
both editions. `test_foundation_wall_height_is_included_in_vertical_continuation`
pins the wall-top depth and rejects a shallow exterior-insulation exception.
`test_internal_source_roof_is_retargeted_without_classifying_ground_floor`
pins the above-ground widening and the ordinary-floor non-leakage control.
`test_unresolved_radiant_floor_does_not_guess_the_ordinary_strip` removes
radiant HVAC from a cloned model, while
`test_real_pipeline_preserves_unresolved_embedded_floor_inventory` exercises
the actual `performance_compliance(simulate="none")` call site; both prove the
pre-teardown inventory prevents the perimeter-strip fallback. The existing
D-32 strip and zone-8 controls remain green.
